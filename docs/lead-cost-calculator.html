What a stale contact list actually costs you

Two ways to get B2B contacts: buy a static database and hope it is still accurate, or pull fresh contacts per use and verify each address over SMTP before you send. The difference shows up as bounce rate — and bounce rate is what burns the domain you send from.

What the numbers mean

LineWhy it matters
Bounced sendsEvery bounce is a wasted send against your sending reputation. Mailbox providers watch the ratio, not your intent.
Usable contactsYou bought N contacts; you can only write to N minus bounces. Databases are sold on records, not on deliverable records.
True cost per usable contactThe number to compare across vendors. It is always higher than the list price you were quoted.
Seat costStatic databases charge per seat per year before you export anything — and you keep paying for stale rows (23% of contacts change jobs annually, ZoomInfo 2025).

Sources for the rates used as defaults: TAPAC bounce 2–5% vs 10–35% for static databases (TAPAC AGENTS.md), 23% of contacts change jobs annually (ZoomInfo 2025), 40% of emails dead within two years (NeverBounce), fresh scraped data outperforms stale databases by 42% (Harvard Business Review 2024).
