When each one is the right answer

Pick Apollo or ZoomInfo if…

You need a full sales platform: sequences, dialer, CRM sync, territory management, and humans working inside a UI all day. You're buying workflow, and contact data is one module of it.

Pick Hunter or Lusha if…

You mainly need to find the email pattern for a domain or a handful of names, and you're fine with credit metering at small volume.

Pick TAPAC if…

An agent is doing the prospecting. You want a verified list back from a tool call, sourced fresh, verified by SMTP at that moment, priced per contact — with no seat licence and no annual commit.

Why verification is the whole ballgame

40% of emails are dead within two years (NeverBounce), and 23% of contacts change jobs every year (ZoomInfo 2025). Any list that was verified when it was built is decaying from that moment. Fresh sourcing plus validation at request time is what keeps a real campaign under a few percent bounce; TAPAC publishes 2–5% against 10–35% for static databases.
